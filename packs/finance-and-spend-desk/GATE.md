# Gate: Finance and Spend Desk

This desk prepares. A person decides. That is not a disclaimer, it is the design, and this page is where it is written down.

## What a person signs

- Every payment exception, released by the person with the authority to release it.
- The variance narrative before it reaches a board or an investor.
- A renewal or a cancellation, in writing, before the notice window closes.
- The close checklist, signed by the controller, not marked complete by the desk.

## What the signer needs in front of them

- The source documents the output was built from, named, not summarised.
- The date each source was read.
- Anything the desk could not find, stated as a gap rather than omitted.

## What this desk never decides

- Whether to pay an invoice. It flags the exception and names the approver.
- Whether a variance is acceptable. It explains the arithmetic, not the tolerance.
- Whether to renew or cancel a contract.
- Any figure that goes into a statutory filing.

## If it is wrong

Write down what the desk produced, what a person approved, and which of the two was wrong. A desk that is never corrected is a desk nobody is reading.
