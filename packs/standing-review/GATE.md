# Gate: Standing Review

This desk prepares. A person decides. That is not a disclaimer, it is the design, and this page is where it is written down.

## What a person signs

- The weekly review itself, read by a person, not filed by one.
- Any decision to retire, pause or change a desk.
- The list of desks currently running, confirmed against reality rather than against this repository.

## What the signer needs in front of them

- The source documents the output was built from, named, not summarised.
- The date each source was read.
- Anything the desk could not find, stated as a gap rather than omitted.

## What this desk never decides

- Whether a desk keeps running. It reports what stopped and who owns it.
- Whether an output was correct. It checks whether an output happened at all.
- Anything about a person's performance. A desk that stopped is an operational fact, not an appraisal.

## If it is wrong

If this review has been green for a month, distrust it. A review that never finds anything is either reading nothing or reading the wrong thing. Check that a deliberately broken routine would show up.
