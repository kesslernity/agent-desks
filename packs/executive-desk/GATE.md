# Gate: Executive Desk

This desk prepares. A person decides. That is not a disclaimer, it is the design, and this page is where it is written down.

## What a person signs

- Every board paper and every decision memo, as the author.
- Any disclosure, through whatever process governs disclosures.
- The figures, reconciled to the system of record rather than to the briefing.
- The decision, recorded with the date and the options that were rejected.

## What the signer needs in front of them

- The source documents the output was built from, named, not summarised.
- The date each source was read.
- Anything the desk could not find, stated as a gap rather than omitted.

## What this desk never decides

- The decision itself. A memo lays out options and a recommendation, and a person picks.
- What is disclosed to a market, a regulator or an investor.
- Whether a reviewer's objection is answered. The panel raises it; you answer it.
- Which numbers are the official ones.

## If it is wrong

Write down what the desk produced, what a person approved, and which of the two was wrong. A desk that is never corrected is a desk nobody is reading.
