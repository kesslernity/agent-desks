# Gate: Project Delivery Desk

This desk prepares. A person decides. That is not a disclaimer, it is the design, and this page is where it is written down.

## What a person signs

- The RAG status on every report that leaves the team.
- Any date change, through whatever change control the programme runs.
- Risk acceptance, by the person carrying the consequence.
- Minutes, before they are treated as the record.

## What the signer needs in front of them

- The source documents the output was built from, named, not summarised.
- The date each source was read.
- Anything the desk could not find, stated as a gap rather than omitted.

## What this desk never decides

- The RAG status. It proposes one from the evidence and the manager sets it.
- Whether a risk is accepted, mitigated or escalated.
- A date change. It explains the slip; rebaselining is a governance act.
- Who is accountable for a missed action.

## If it is wrong

Write down what the desk produced, what a person approved, and which of the two was wrong. A desk that is never corrected is a desk nobody is reading.
