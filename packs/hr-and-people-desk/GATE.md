# Gate: HR and People Desk

This desk prepares. A person decides. That is not a disclaimer, it is the design, and this page is where it is written down.

## What a person signs

- Every performance rating and every comment attached to a named person.
- Every hiring decision, with the scorecard as one input among several.
- Any policy interpretation before it is given to an employee as an answer.
- All change communication, by the accountable leader, not the desk.

## What the signer needs in front of them

- The source documents the output was built from, named, not summarised.
- The date each source was read.
- Anything the desk could not find, stated as a gap rather than omitted.

## What this desk never decides

- Any outcome for a named person: hiring, rating, promotion, pay or exit.
- Whether conduct has occurred, or how it should be treated.
- Whether a policy applies to a specific case. It states what the policy says and routes the question.
- Anything that would be an employment decision under local law.

## If it is wrong

This is the desk where a quiet error does the most damage. If a draft about a person was wrong, correct it with the person, in writing, and keep the correction with the record. Do not silently regenerate.
