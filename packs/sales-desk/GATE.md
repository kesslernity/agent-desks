# Gate: Sales Desk

This desk prepares. A person decides. That is not a disclaimer, it is the design, and this page is where it is written down.

## What a person signs

- Anything that reaches a customer, read in full first.
- The commit, by the account owner, in the forecast review.
- Every commercial term, through whatever delegation of authority applies.
- A case study, by the customer, before it is published.

## What the signer needs in front of them

- The source documents the output was built from, named, not summarised.
- The date each source was read.
- Anything the desk could not find, stated as a gap rather than omitted.

## What this desk never decides

- Whether to bid. A requirements pack informs the bid or no-bid meeting.
- Price, discount or commercial terms.
- A forecast commit. Qualification scoring is evidence for the conversation.
- What is said to a customer. Every draft goes out under a person's name, after they have read it.

## If it is wrong

Write down what the desk produced, what a person approved, and which of the two was wrong. A desk that is never corrected is a desk nobody is reading.
