# Gate: Security and GRC Desk

This desk prepares. A person decides. That is not a disclaimer, it is the design, and this page is where it is written down.

## What a person signs

- Every control effectiveness conclusion, with the evidence reference.
- Any regulator-facing statement, before it leaves the organisation.
- The residual risk rating and the acceptance, by the risk owner.
- A vendor approval, with the screening brief attached rather than quoted.

## What the signer needs in front of them

- The source documents the output was built from, named, not summarised.
- The date each source was read.
- Anything the desk could not find, stated as a gap rather than omitted.

## What this desk never decides

- Whether a control is effective. It gathers evidence and states what is missing.
- Whether an incident is reportable to a regulator. It drafts the impact brief and stops.
- Whether a vendor is acceptable. A screening brief is an input to a decision, not the decision.
- The residual risk rating on any register line.

## If it is wrong

A gap this desk missed is worth more than a gap it found. Log the miss against the control, dated, and put the check that would have caught it into the next pack.
