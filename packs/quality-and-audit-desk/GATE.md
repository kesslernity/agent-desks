# Gate: Quality and Audit Desk

This desk prepares. A person decides. That is not a disclaimer, it is the design, and this page is where it is written down.

## What a person signs

- Every nonconformance closure, by the quality manager.
- Effectiveness verification, with the evidence, not the intention.
- Every controlled document revision, by its custodian, through the document control system.
- Every safety authorisation, by an authorised person, with no agent output in the approval path.

## What the signer needs in front of them

- The source documents the output was built from, named, not summarised.
- The date each source was read.
- Anything the desk could not find, stated as a gap rather than omitted.

## What this desk never decides

- Whether a nonconformance is closed.
- Whether a corrective action was effective.
- Any safety authorisation. Not a permit to work, not a lockout, not a confined space entry, not a JSA sign-off, not an inspection clearance.
- The revision status of a controlled document.

## If it is wrong

In a regulated environment a wrong draft that was approved is a finding about the approval, not about the draft. Record both: what the desk produced and who approved it.
